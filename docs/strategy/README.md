## Conventions

- Record strategy decisions in `DECISIONS.md` with date and who decided.
- Claims in marketing materials must be true today. Say "pilot" when it's a pilot;
  don't claim approvals (e.g., BYU security review) until they're granted.
- No student data, names, or real transcripts in marketing assets without consent.
