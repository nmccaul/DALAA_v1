Two questions sort the market: does the class prohibit or allow AI, and is it a technical or non-technical class? Prohibiting AI means detectors and proctoring like Honorlock and Proctorio, and in technical classes, code-recording plugins too. Technical classes that allow AI have real tools: students build with Claude Code or Codex, and using them well is the skill. Non-technical classes that allow AI have nothing but "report how you used AI," and in reality students just let AI complete everything. That's the sector DALAA fixes.
