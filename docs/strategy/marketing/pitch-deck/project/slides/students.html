Value to students

Students learn to use AI well — and learn more.

Learn AI skills

Practice working with AI the right way: question it, defend a position to it, explain ideas to it — skills they'll use at work.

Amplify learning

Active practice instead of passive reading, with immediate feedback and another try until it clicks.

For students the AI is a sparring partner, not a shortcut. They build the habit of using AI with structure, and they learn the material more deeply because they have to explain and defend it.
