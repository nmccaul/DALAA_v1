Value to students

AI becomes a sparring partner, not a shortcut.

Learn AI skills

Question it, defend a position to it, explain ideas to it: the way they'll use AI at work.

Learn the material more deeply

Active practice instead of passive reading, with feedback and another try until it clicks.

For students the AI is a sparring partner, not a shortcut. They build the habit of using AI with structure, and they learn the material more deeply because they have to explain and defend it.
