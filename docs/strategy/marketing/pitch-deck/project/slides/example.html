Example: a Case Chat

Students question the case's CEO.

1 · Read the case

Should a coffee chain raise prices?

Costs are up and visits are down. The CEO wants advice.

2 · Question the CEO

Why didn't you raise prices when costs went up?

Our regulars watch prices closely, so I cut staff hours instead.

Did wait times change after that?

They went up. I hadn't connected that to fewer visits.

Findings uncovered: 2 of 4

3 · Take a position

“Restore staffing before touching prices.”

The student defends it to the CEO. The teacher sees every position and the reasoning behind it.

A sample Case Chat, made up for this slide. The student reads a short case, then interviews its CEO, played by AI, who reveals facts only when asked the right questions.
